Riverford District Council: Internal Audit of the Road Resurfacing Programme, 2025-26
(Fictional document created for testing Groundline.)

Summary

The council budgeted 14.2 million for road resurfacing in 2025-26. By the end of March 2026 it had spent 11.8 million, or 83 per cent of the budget. Of the 212 road sections planned, 164 were completed.

The audit reviewed 40 contracts. In nine of them, payment was released before the work was inspected. The audit team said this may indicate weak controls, but found no evidence that any payment was made for work that was not done.

Contractors

Three contractors received 71% of the programme's spending. The largest, Halden Surfacing Ltd, received 4.9 million across 18 contracts. The audit noted that two contracts awarded to Halden Surfacing were extended without a fresh tender, which is allowed under the council's rules for extensions below 10 per cent of the contract value.

Quality

Independent tests of 25 completed sections found that 6 did not meet the required surface thickness. The council's head of highways, Priya Nandakumar, said: "We have asked the contractors to repair the failed sections at their own cost, and those repairs are scheduled for June."

Recommendations

The audit recommends that no payment be released before a signed inspection report is on file, and that the council publish a list of all contract extensions every quarter. The council has accepted both recommendations and expects to apply them from July 2026.
