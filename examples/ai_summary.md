# Riverford road audit: summary

Riverford District Council budgeted 14.2 million for road resurfacing in 2025-26 and spent 11.8 million by the end of March 2026.

Of the 212 road sections planned, 146 were completed.

The audit reviewed 40 contracts and found that in nine of them payment was released before the work was inspected.

This proves the council paid for work that was never done.

Three contractors received 71% of the spending, and the largest, Halden Surfacing Ltd, received 4.9 million.

Priya Nandakumar said: "We will make the contractors pay for every failed road in the district."

Local residents have complained about potholes for years, according to the Riverford Gazette.

The council has accepted both recommendations and expects to apply them from July 2026.
